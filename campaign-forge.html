Forge recipe

  1. Read the Brand Brain.Identity, voice, facts, rules, audience, products, and learned feedback.
  2. Check existing campaigns.Avoid accidental duplicates and preserve what is already working.
  3. Inspect the destination.When you provide a public URL, Ember checks the actual offer page.
  4. Generate the package.5 hooks, 3 campaign concepts, copy, CTA, audience direction, and creative brief.
  5. Write it home.One draft campaign plus your selected number of draft content items are saved in BSMS.
Iron gate:

Campaign Forge may create drafts. It does not publish, schedule, send messages, change budgets, or spend money.

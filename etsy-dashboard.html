    Performance

    What the numbers mean

    The sales summary reports recent paid receipts from the Etsy API. Any calculated subtotal is a gross merchandise figure, not revenue deposited or profit. It excludes shipping and taxes and is not adjusted for every refund, discount, or Etsy fee. When the report is incomplete or some subtotals are unavailable, you'll see a notice instead of an invented total.

# Stag & Stone — First 30-Day Pre-Opening Content Rhythm

Status: editorial plan, not a dated schedule.

This is a sequence-based 30-day rhythm. Day numbers are relative positions only. Do not convert them into calendar dates until Farmer explicitly chooses a start date.

## Cadence

Aim for 3 primary posts per 7-day block, with lighter Story/update opportunities between them when genuine progress exists. Silence is better than manufactured progress.

## Days 1–7 — Establish the World

Day 1 — Brand introduction
- Pillar: opening journey / identity
- Goal: awareness
- Message: Stag & Stone is taking shape in Clarkesville.
- Approved language available: “Crafted for the morning ritual.”
- Do not imply the cafe is open.

Day 3 — Morning ritual
- Pillar: morning ritual / coffee
- Goal: brand feeling
- Focus: what a takeaway-first coffee-and-bakehouse morning can feel like.
- Avoid invented menu specifics.

Day 5 — Bakehouse introduction
- Pillar: bakehouse
- Goal: offering awareness
- Confirmed scope: breakfast, lunch, breads, pastries, daily biscuits, rotating cakes/pastries.
- Do not present menu as finalized.

Day 7 — Genuine progress update
- Pillar: behind the scenes / opening journey
- Use only progress that actually happened that week.
- If there is no meaningful progress, skip rather than manufacture an update.

## Days 8–14 — Show the Range

Day 9 — Coffee Cart
- Pillar: mobile services
- Goal: event-service awareness
- Confirmed: markets, festivals, private events; coffee and nonalcoholic beverage service.
- No invented packages, prices, availability, or service area.

Day 11 — Lynn’s Custom Bakes
- Pillar: bakehouse / service
- Goal: distinguish custom bakes from everyday pastry-case offerings.
- No invented flavors, designs, prices, lead times, or availability.

Day 13 — Community vision
- Pillar: community
- Goal: communicate intended gathering culture.
- May mention planned MTG, D&D, and monthly book club.
- Do not advertise dates/times until confirmed.

Day 14 — Behind-the-scenes detail
- Pillar: people/process
- Use a real build, recipe, equipment, signage, cart, or planning moment.

## Days 15–21 — Deepen the Brand

Day 16 — Appalachian / North Georgia sense of place
- Pillar: atmosphere
- Goal: reinforce regional identity naturally.
- Never invent sourcing, family heritage, historical recipes, or generational claims.

Day 18 — Mobile Tavern
- Pillar: mobile services
- Confirmed boundary: client purchases and transports all alcohol; Stag & Stone provides mobile setup/service within confirmed scope.
- Never imply alcohol is included or sold/transported by Stag & Stone.

Day 20 — The Tall Man development
- Pillar: people/process / house drinks
- It is a signature drink under development: iced latte with espresso, dark maple, vanilla, extremely subtle smoked sea salt note.
- Must remain framed as development, not a finalized/currently available menu item.

Day 21 — Progress reflection
- Pillar: opening journey
- Summarize only genuine progress that has occurred.

## Days 22–30 — Build Familiarity

Day 23 — Takeaway-first service model
- Pillar: practical brand education
- Explain that Stag & Stone is being developed as takeaway-first with dine-in seating available.
- Do not call it full-service sit-down or grab-and-go-only.

Day 25 — Community-night spotlight
- Choose book club, MTG, or D&D.
- Discuss the concept/intent, not an event announcement unless scheduling is confirmed.

Day 27 — Food & drink category spotlight
- Choose one confirmed category: coffee/espresso, breakfast, lunch, breads/pastries, house drinks/apothecary.
- Do not invent individual finalized items.

Day 29 — People/process
- Real owner/build/bake/coffee/process moment.
- Favor human-scale detail over hype.

Day 30 — Month-one reflection / invitation
- Reflect on what was actually revealed or accomplished during the sequence.
- CTA: follow along as Stag & Stone takes shape.
- Do not promise an opening date unless separately confirmed.

## Opportunistic posts

When these milestones actually occur, they may interrupt the rhythm:
- lease signed
- sign installed
- renovations started
- espresso equipment arrives
- first recipe test
- menu finalized
- staff introduction
- health inspection passed
- opening date confirmed
- soft opening
- grand opening

Their current database status is planned and undated. They are triggers, not scheduled content.

## Asset alignment

Existing community-night banners can support future book club, D&D, and MTG content once the relevant asset is approved for marketing and the post does not imply unconfirmed event details.

## Rule

Real progress beats content cadence. Never create a fictional milestone merely to fill a slot.

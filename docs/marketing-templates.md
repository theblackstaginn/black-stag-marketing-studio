# Marketing Studio Reusable Templates

## Campaign Brief

- Name
- Brand
- Objective
- Business context
- Target audience(s)
- Verified offer/product/service
- Key message
- Supporting Brand Brain facts
- Channels
- Voice notes
- CTA
- Start/end dates if confirmed
- Constraints/guardrails
- Success signals
- Status: draft by default

## Social Content Brief

- Brand
- Campaign
- Platform
- Content type
- Goal
- Audience
- Verified facts allowed in copy
- Voice direction
- Hook direction
- CTA
- Visual direction
- Required asset(s)
- Date/time only if confirmed
- Status: draft by default

## Launch / Announcement Checklist

- Is the underlying event/product/status confirmed?
- Is the date confirmed?
- Is availability confirmed?
- Are price claims verified?
- Are ingredient/sourcing claims verified when applicable?
- Is the visual approved_for_marketing?
- Does copy comply with Brand Brain rules?
- Has content been explicitly approved?
- Is scheduling explicit and correct?

## Asset Creative Brief

- Brand
- Campaign/content relationship
- Intended placement
- Dimensions/aspect ratio
- Subject
- Required brand elements
- Material/style direction
- Text to render, if any
- Accessibility/alt-text intent
- Variants required
- Approval state

## Post-Campaign Review

- Objective
- What shipped
- Confirmed results
- What worked
- What did not
- Audience/channel observations
- Reusable assets/content
- Lessons
- Brand Brain updates proposed
- Follow-up actions

Results and lessons must remain distinct from assumptions.
